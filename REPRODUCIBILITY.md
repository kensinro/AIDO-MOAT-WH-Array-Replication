# Reproducibility contract

The reference package is deliberately narrower than a full WH implementation.

## Included
- frozen result-level evidence;
- independent metric recomputation;
- provenance/hash validation;
- comparator and transfer scoring rules;
- Human-Gated R³ closure verification;
- regression tests against manuscript headline values.

## Excluded
- hidden development generators not needed to check the published results;
- complete future-facing WH Core implementation;
- production execution/repair authority;
- Optimize/Design modules.

## Non-retroactivity
- Protocol 1's NOVEL topology result remains 0/20 and is not backfilled by Protocol 2.
- The original comparator HOLD remains part of provenance; the fresh P3-A2R replication is the manuscript's primary comparator evidence.
